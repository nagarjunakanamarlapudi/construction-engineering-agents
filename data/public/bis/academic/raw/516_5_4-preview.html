ICS 91.100.30 CED 02

New Standard from Last Update.

1 SCOPE

This standard (Part 5/Sec 4) covers the objective, principle, apparatus and procedure of rebound hammer test for determination of the rebound number of hardened concrete using a spring-driven steel hammer. In addition, influence of test conditions and some general guidance on the interpretation of test results are also given.

NOTE â€” The test method is not intended as an alternative for the compressive strength determination of concrete; but with suitable correlation, it can provide an estimate of in-situ strength.
