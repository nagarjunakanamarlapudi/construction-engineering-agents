IS 8009 : Part 2 : 1980 : Code of practice for calculation of settlement of foundations: Part 2 Deep foundations subjected to symmetrical static vertical loading

UDC 624.15 : 624.131.526 : 006.76 CED 43
Reaffirmed 2020

1. Scope

1.1 This Standard(Part II) provides simple methods for the estimation of elastic compression of the foundation structure, immediate and primary consolidation settlements of deep foundations under symmetrical static vertical loads. In addition, procedures for computing time rate of settlement are also given
