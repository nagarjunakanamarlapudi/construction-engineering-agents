IS 516 : Part 2 : Sec 1 : 2018 Hardened Concrete — Methods of Test Part 2 Properties of Hardened Concrete other than Strength Section 1 Density of Hardened Concrete and Depth of Water Penetration Under Pressure

ICS 91.100.30

CED 2

Reaffirmed 2023

1. SCOPE

This standard (Part 2/Section 1) covers procedures for testing properties of hardened concrete other than strength, namely density of hardened concrete and depth of water penetration under pressure.
