IS 2720 : Part 30 : 1980 Methods of test for soils: Part 30 Laboratory vane shear test

UDC 624.131.377 CED 43
Reaffirmed 2021

1. SCOPE

1.1 This standard (Part XXX) covers the procedure of conducting laboratory vane shear test on cohesive soils of low shear strength for determining their undrained shear strength.
