IS 516 : Part 5 : Sec 3 : 2021 Hardened Concrete - Methods of Test Part 5 Non-Destructive Testing of Concrete Section 3 Carbonation Depth Test

ICS 91.100.30                         CED 02

New Standard from Last Update.

1 SCOPE

This standard IS 516 (Part 5/Sec 3) specifies a nondestructive test method for use on hardened concrete, namely determination of carbonation depth.
