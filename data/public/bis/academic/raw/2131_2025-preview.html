IS 2131 : 2025 Standard Penetration Test of Soil - Method of Test

ICS 93.020

CED 43

1 SCOPE

1.1 This standard specifies a standard procedure for execution and reporting of the standard penetration test of soil.

1.1.1 The standard penetration test (SPT) aims to determine the resistance of soils at any depth in a borehole/trial pit to the penetration of a standard split spoon sampler and to recover disturbed samples for identification purposes.

1.1.2 The basis of the test consists of driving a split spoon sampler by dropping a hammer of 63.5 kg ± 0.5 kg mass on to an anvil or drive head from a height of 750 mm ± 10 mm. The number of blows (N) necessary to achieve 300 mm penetration of the sampler (after a seating penetration of 150 mm including self-penetration under gravity) is the penetration resistance.

1.2 SPT is used mainly to assess the strength and deformation parameters of cohesionless soils, but some valuable data may also be obtained in other soil types.
