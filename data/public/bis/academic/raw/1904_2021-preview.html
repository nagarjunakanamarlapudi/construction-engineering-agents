IS 1904 : 2021 General Requirements for Design and Construction of Foundations in Soils - Code of Practice

ICS 93.020

CED 43

Revised Standard from Last Update.

1. Scope

This standard covers the general requirements for design and construction of all types of foundations (shallow, deep and special types).

2. References

The standards given in Annex A contain provisions, which through reference in this text, constitute provisions of this standard. At the time of publication the editions indicated are valid. All standards are subject to revision, and parties to agreement based on this standard are encouraged to investigate the possibility of applying the most recent editions of the standards indicated in Annex A.
