IS 808 : 2021 Hot Rolled Steel Beam, Column, Channel and Angle Sections - Dimensions and Properties

ICS 77.140.01

CED 07

Revised Standard from Last Update.

1. SCOPE

This standard covers the nominal dimensions, mass and sectional properties of hot rolled sloping and parallel flange beam and column sections, sloping and parallel flange channel sections, equal and unequal leg angle sections, and parallel flange bearing piles.
