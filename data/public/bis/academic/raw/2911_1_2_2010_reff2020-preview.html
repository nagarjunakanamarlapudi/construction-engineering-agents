IS 2911 : Part 1 : Sec 2 : 2010 : Design And Construction Of Pile Foundations - Code Of Practice Part 1 Concrete Piles Section 2 Bored Cast In-situ Concrete Piles

ICS 91.100.30 : 93.020 CED 43
Reaffirmed 2020

1 SCOPE

1.1 This standard (Part 1/Sec 2) covers the design and construction of bored cast in-situ concrete piles which transmit the load to the soil by resistance developed either at the pile tip by end-bearing or along the surface of the shaft by friction or by both.

1.2 This standard is not applicable for use of bored cast in-situ concrete piles for any other purpose, for example, temporary or permanent retaining structure.
