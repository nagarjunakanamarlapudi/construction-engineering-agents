IS 383 : 2016 Coarse and Fine Aggregate for Concrete - Specification

ICS 91.100.30                         CED 02

1. Scope

This standard covers the requirements for aggregates, crushed or uncrushed, derived from natural sources, such as river terraces and riverbeds, glacial deposits, rocks, boulers and gravels, and manufactured aggregates produced from other than natural sources, for use in the production of concrete for normal structural purposes inculding mass concrete works.
