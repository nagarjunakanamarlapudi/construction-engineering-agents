IS 6403 : 1981 Code of practice for determination of bearing capacity of shallow foundations

UDC 624.151.5 : 624.131.52 : 006.76 CED 43
Reaffirmed 2021

1 Scope

1.1 This standard covers the procedure for determining theultimate bearing capacity and allowable bearing pressure of shallowfoundations based on shear and allowable settlement criteria.
