IS 516 : Part 11 : 2020 Hardened Concrete-Methods of Test - Part 11 Determination of Portland Cement Content of Hardened Hydraulic Cement Concrete

ICS 91.100.30 CED 02

New Standard from Last Update.

1 SCOPE

1.1 This standard (Part 11) specifies the procedures for determination of Portland cement content of a sample of hardened hydraulic cement concrete. The test method is applicable only for concrete made with ordinary Portland cement.
