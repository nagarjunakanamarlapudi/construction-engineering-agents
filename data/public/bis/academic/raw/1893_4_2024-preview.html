IS 1893 : Part 4 : 2024 Criteria for Earthquake Resistant Design of Structures Part 4 Industrial Structures Including Stack- Like Structures

ICS 91.120.25                          CED 39

1. Scope

1.1 The industrial structures, including stack-like structures, shall be designed and constructed to resist the earthquake effects in accordance with the requirements and provisions of this standard. This standard describes the procedures for earthquake resistant design and provides the estimates of earthquake forces for design of such structures.

1.2 Temporary elements such as scaffolding, temporary excavations need not be designed for earthquake forces.

1.3 This standard does not deal with construction features relating to earthquake resistant industrial structures and stack like structures. For guidance on earthquake resistant construction of buildings, reference may be made to the following Indian Standards: IS 4326, IS 13827, IS 13828, IS 13920 and IS 13935.

1.4 This standard deals with earthquake resistant design of the industrial structures (plant and auxiliary structures) including stack like structures. This standard shall be considered applicable to all industries, including those listed below, unless exclusively noted otherwise:

a) Process industries;

b) Power plants;

c) Petroleum, fertilizers, chemical and petrochemical industries;

d) Steel, copper, zinc and aluminum plants;

e) Pharmaceutical plants;

f) Cement industries;

g) Automobile industries;

h) Sugar and alcohol industries;

j) Glass and ceramic industries;

k) Textile industries;

m) Foundries;

n) Electrical and electronic industries;

p) Consumer product industries;

q) Structures for water and waste water treatment

plants and pump houses;

r) Leather industries;

s) Off-shore structures and marine/port/harbour structures;

t) Mill structures;

u) Telephone exchanges; and

v) Paper plants.

NOTE - For nuclear and other hazardous chemical installations, reference may also be made to recommendations of applicable International Safety Standards.

In addition to the above, the following structures, classi?ed as stack like structures, are also covered by this standard:

1) Cooling towers and prilling towers;

2) Transmission and communication towers/masts;

3) Chimneys and stack like structures;

4) Silos (including parabolic silos used for urea storage) ;

5) Support structures for re?nery columns; and

6) Pressure vessels and chemical reactor columns.
