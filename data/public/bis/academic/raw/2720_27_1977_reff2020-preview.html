IS 2720 : Part 27 : 1977 Methods of test for soils: Part 27 Determination of total soluble sulphates

UDC 624.131.41 : 543.83 : 546.226 CED 43
Reaffirmed 2020

1. SCOPE

1.1 This standard ( Part XXVII) lays down the procedure for determining the total soluble sulphate content of soils by: (a) precipitation method, (b) volumetric method, and (c) colorimetric or turbidimetric method.
