IS 9595 : 1996 Metal arc welding of carbon and carbon manganese steels - Recommendations

ICS 25.160 : 77.140 MTD 11
Reaffirmed 2019

1 Scope

1.1 This standard recommends general requirements for the manual,semi-automatic and automatic metal arc welding of carbon and carbon manganesesteel in all product forms including tubes and hollow sections, complying withthe requirements of 5.

1.2 The annexures are intended to assist users of this Standard by givingguidance on various topics.
