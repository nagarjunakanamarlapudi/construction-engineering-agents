IS 2720 : Part 1 : 1983 Methods of Test for Soils - Part 1 : Preparation of Dry Soil Samples for Various Tests

UDC 624.131.37 CED 43
Reaffirmed 2020

1 Scope

1.1 This standard (Part 1) covers the method of preparation of dry samples from the bulk soil sample received from the field forvarious laboratory tests.
