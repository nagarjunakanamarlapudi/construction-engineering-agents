IS 4926 : 2003 Ready-Mixed Concrete - Code of Practice

ICS 91.100.30 CED 2
Reaffirmed 2022

1 Scope

This standard covers the requirements for the production andsupply of ready-mixed concrete. It does not cover the placing,compaction, curing or protection of concrete after delivery.

2 References

The standards given in Annex A contain provisionswhich through reference in this text, constituteprovisions of this standard. At the time of publication,the editions indicated were valid. All standards aresubject to revision, and parties to agreements based onthis standard are encouraged to investigate thepossibility of applying the most recent editions of thestandards indicated in Annex A.
