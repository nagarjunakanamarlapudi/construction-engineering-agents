IS 2911 : Part 1 : Sec 1 : 2010 : Design And Construction Of Pile Foundations - Code Of Practice Part 1 Concrete Piles Section 1 Driven Cast In-situ Concrete Piles

ICS 91.100.30 : 93.020 CED 43
Reaffirmed 2020

1 SCOPE

1.1 This standard (Part 1/Sec 1) covers the design and construction of driven cast in-situ concrete piles which transmit the load to the soil by resistance developed either at the pile tip by endbearing or along the surface of the shaft by friction or by both.

1.2 This standard is not applicable for use of driven cast in-situ concrete piles for any other purpose, for example, temporary or permanent retaining structure.
