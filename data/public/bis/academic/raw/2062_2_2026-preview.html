IS 2062 : Part 2 : 2026 Structural Steel - Part 2 Hot Rolled Quenched and Tempered Steel Plates, Sheets, Strips and Wide Flats

ICS 77.140.01

MTD 04

1 SCOPE

1.1 This standard specifies qualities for high-yield strength quenched and tempered structural steels.

1.2 It applies to steel plates, sheets, strips and wide flats rolled on reversing mills which are used in the quenched and tempered condition and normally intended for welded or bolted structures.

1.3 The steels specified in this document are applicable to hot-rolled flat products with a minimum nominal thickness of 3 mm and a maximum nominal thickness of:

a) 200 mm for grades ISH S460Q, ISH S500Q, ISH S550Q, ISH S620Q and ISH S690Q;

b) 125 mm for grades ISH S890Q and ISH S960Q;

c) 100 mm for grades ISH SG460Q and ISH SG500Q; and

d) 150 mm for grade ISH SG700Q.

NOTE - Steel below 3 mm thickness may be supplied if mutually agreed during enquiry and order, but shall not be less than 2 mm.
