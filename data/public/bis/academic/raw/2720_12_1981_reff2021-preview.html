IS 2720 : Part 12 : 1981 Methods of test for soils: Part 12 Determination of shear strength parameters of soil from consolidated undrained triaxial compression test with measurement of pore water pressure

UDC 624.131.439.5 : 624.131.536 CED 43
Reaffirmed 2021

1. SCOPE

1.1 This standard ( Part XII ) lays down the method of determining the shear strength parameters of saturated soils from triaxial compression shear tests conducted under consolidated undrained conditions with measurements of pore water pressures. It also describes a procedure.

1.2 The test is limited to specimens in the form of right cylinders of nominal diameter 38,50,70 or 100 mm with height twice its diameter. The ratio of diameter of the sample to the maximum size of the particle in the soil shall not be less than five.
