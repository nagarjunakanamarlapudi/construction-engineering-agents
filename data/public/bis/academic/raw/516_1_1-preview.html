IS 516 : Part 1 : Sec 1 : 2021 Hardened Concrete - Methods of Test - Part 1 Testing of Strength of Hardened Concrete -Section 1 Compressive, Flexural and Split Tensile Strength

ICS 91.100.30 CED 02

New Standard from Last Update.

1 SCOPE

This standard (Part 1/Section 1) covers procedures for testing compressive, flexural and split tensile strength of hardened concrete.
